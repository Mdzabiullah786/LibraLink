About LibraLink

Bridging the gap between libraries and knowledge seekers

🏛️

LibraLink is an innovative interlibrary borrowing management system designed to connect students and researchers with the books they need, regardless of which library holds them.

Our platform streamlines the entire borrowing process — from discovering available titles across partner libraries to submitting requests, tracking shipments, and managing returns.

Whether you're a student looking for a rare textbook or a library managing incoming requests, LibraLink makes interlibrary borrowing simple and efficient.
